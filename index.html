Can a video model remember what it can no longer see? WROP studies whether models preserve object identity through occlusion and respect the solidity of physical objects.

arXivWebsiteCodeDataModelBenchmarkLeaderboard
Research overview

WROP brings together 150 cognitively grounded 3D tasks, a 1.5M-sample corpus, a fixed 300-question exam, a human evaluation framework, and PWM-WROP, a fine-tuned 16B continuation model. These are collaborative resources developed by the author team.
