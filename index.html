WROP · World Reasoning with Object Permanence

Haotian Zhang, Fengyuan Yu, Dezhi Luo, Haoran Sun, Zehong Zhao, Qingying Gao, et al.

Can video models keep track of objects they can no longer see? WROP connects cognitively grounded task design, scalable synthetic data, and human evaluation to study object permanence and solidity in video generation.

Research overviewProblem, approach & resources+
The problem
A video can look plausible frame by frame while losing an object’s identity or violating physical constraints. WROP studies whether models preserve objects through occlusion and respect the solidity of physical objects.
The approach
The project brings together 150 cognitively grounded 3D tasks and a Blender generator for each task. Each generator can produce 10,000+ diverse samples while preserving the task’s cognitive structure.
Training & evaluation
The collaborative release includes a 1.5M-sample corpus, a fixed 300-question exam, a human evaluation framework, and PWM-WROP, a fine-tuned 16B continuation model. These are project-level resources developed by the author team.
