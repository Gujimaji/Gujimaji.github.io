Haotian Zhang 张皓天

I am a first-year undergraduate student at the University of Southern California (USC), majoring in Applied and Computational Mathematics.

My research focuses on World Models, Physical Reasoning, and Video Generation. I am broadly interested in how generative models can move beyond merely synthesizing visually plausible pixels toward internalizing structured physical laws—such as object permanence, solidity, and spatio-temporal continuity.

By bridging rigorous mathematical modeling, large-scale 3D synthetic simulation, and cognitive science paradigms, I aim to make physical intelligence in foundation world models measurable, trainable, and mathematically grounded.

Training Object Permanence in World Models

Haotian Zhang*, Fengyuan Yu, Dezhi Luo, Haoran Sun, Zehong Zhao, Qingying Gao, ... Yihan Li, Siyuan An, Huayi Qin, Yilan Zhang, Zhengze Jiang, Pinyuan Feng, Renrui Zhang, Ziyu Guo, Letian Wang, Mengyue Yang, Kangfu Mei, Maijunxian Wang, Ran Ji, Vikash Kumar, Freda Shi, Chandra Sripada, Vincent C. Müller, Philip Torr, Alan Yuille, Nikolaus Kriegeskorte, Felix Juefei-Xu, Lvmin Zhang, Jieneng Chen, Yilun Du, Hokin Deng

Current video generation models can synthesize visually plausible frames while losing track of an occluded object's identity, location, or physical consequences. We introduce WROP (World Reasoning with Object Permanence), a full-stack training and evaluation resource for object permanence and solidity in world models.

🌐 Website 📄 Paper 💻 Code 🗂️ Training Data (1.5M) 🧠 Model (PWM-WROP 16B) 📊 Leaderboard
01 · 150 Cognitive 3D Tasks

Hand-designed procedural Blender task generators across six core cognitive science families, each scalable to 10K+ diverse samples.

02 · 1.5M Training Corpus & Exam

1.5M video-continuation training samples paired with a fixed 300-question evaluation exam and blind pairwise human Elo benchmark across 14 models.

03 · PWM-WROP (16B)

A fine-tuned 16B continuation world model trained on our corpus, ranking #1 among continuation models in blind pairwise human Elo evaluation.

Research Vision

I · Physical World Models

Beyond Pixel Plausibility

Developing generative video and world models that maintain coherent internal state representations under long-horizon occlusion, physical collision, and counterfactual interventions.

II · Cognitive & Procedural Scaling

Structured Synthetic Environments

Translating developmental cognitive science paradigms into controllable, large-scale 3D simulation factories that isolate and train fundamental physical reasoning primitives.

III · Mathematical Foundations

Rigorous Evaluation & Dynamics

Leveraging applied and computational mathematics to formalize world state estimation, dynamical consistency, and scalable training architectures.
